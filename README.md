# Best Time for AI — Low-Traffic Planner for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini & DeepSeek

Live interactive web tool: **[Free AI tools: when Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek respond faster — in your time zone](https://24navobot.pythonanywhere.com/en/free_neural_networks_best-time-for-ai)**

## Overview
**Best Time for AI** is a browser-based timezone planner that helps users find low-traffic hours ("golden windows") when popular AI models—including **Claude AI**, **ChatGPT**, **Google Gemini**, and **DeepSeek**—respond faster and consume free-tier rolling limits more efficiently.

The model maps typical North American West Coast (San Francisco, `UTC-7` / `UTC-8`) business and leisure traffic cycles onto any selected `UTC−12` to `UTC+14` timezone.

## Key Features
- **Automatic & Manual Timezone Conversion**: Auto-detects your local browser timezone or lets you switch across any `UTC−12` to `UTC+14` zone.
- **US Daylight Saving Toggle**: Switch between US Summer Time (`UTC-7`, PDT) and US Winter Time (`UTC-8`, PST).
- **Interactive Load Dial & "Time Machine" Slider**: Preview estimated server load (`0–100%` heuristic scale) for any hour of the day.
- **Weekday vs. Weekend/Holiday Profiles**: Separate demand curves accounting for lower corporate/developer traffic on weekends and US holidays.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Full support for English (`EN`) and Russian (`RU`) with dark/light theme switching.

## How It Helps
- **Golden Window (Low Load)**: Ideal for heavy tasks, long contexts, large document uploads, and multi-step agentic workflows.
- **Transition Hours**: Moderate demand; suitable for standard prompts with a small buffer for latency.
- **US Peak Hours**: High competition for GPU/TPU compute; best suited for short, targeted queries.
